**FM Linear integrates [Firstmate](https://github.com/kunchenguid/firstmate) with [Linear](https://linear.app).**
It brings the work your agents are doing into Linear, where you can follow progress, leave comments, and approve next steps.

Firstmate gives you one agent to talk to while it coordinates a crew across your repositories.
As that work grows, tracking it across conversations becomes difficult.
What is still in progress?
Which plan needs a review?
Where did the crew leave the result?

FM Linear is an independent integration for Firstmate that keeps those requests, updates, and decisions together in Linear issues.
Ask Firstmate for work as usual, then watch the crew's progress and respond in Linear when Firstmate needs your input.
Your team chooses the statuses, people, labels, and review requirements that make the workflow useful.

## What you do in Linear

- **Find your work.** Requests you make to Firstmate are connected to issues in your chosen team.
- **Follow progress.** See the work stage, the crewmate involved, and who owes the next action.
- **Give direction.** Reply to questions, review plans, and approve a specific next step in the issue's conversation.
- **Find the result.** Open the current plan, pull request, review guide, and other artifacts from the issue.

For example, you might request team invitations for your app.
Firstmate delegates the work, a crewmate prepares the change, and FM Linear puts the issue in your review queue with the relevant links.
You reply in the review thread; Firstmate receives that feedback and coordinates the next step.

Read [Affiliation](/affiliation/) for our relationship to Firstmate and what “plugin” means on this site.

## Firstmate still runs the crew

Firstmate decides how to break down requests, schedule work, assign crewmates, and supervise execution.
FM Linear connects that work to your Linear workflow and keeps the conversation moving between the two systems.

You still use Linear's familiar issues and views.
There is no separate FM Linear project-management dashboard to keep up to date.

## Your workflow stays yours

Start with a suggested workflow or map your existing statuses and labels.
Choose who reviews each kind of work and what the crew should deliver before asking for approval.

You can change those choices as your process evolves.
For example, require an interactive review guide for implementation work, but a written summary for an investigation.
