## Motivation

You want to review the crew's proposed approach before it starts implementation.
In the recommended workflow, the issue is in **Approve Plan** and is assigned to you as the captain, or to another reviewer selected in your configuration.

## How to do it

Open the plan linked from the Linear issue.
Check that its scope, constraints, and acceptance criteria match your request.
The review conversation should identify the plan version, explain important tradeoffs, and surface any open questions.

If the approach needs revision, reply in the review thread with your feedback.
If you are ready to proceed, approve the specific plan:

> I approve version 2 of this plan for implementation.
> Keep the proposed scope; we will review the deliverable before merging.

You can answer questions and give approval in the same reply when the scope is clear.
If requested changes make your approval ambiguous, Firstmate asks for clarification before proceeding.

## What happens

Firstmate receives your decision with the plan it concerns.
An authorized approval of the current plan permits implementation of that plan.
If you request revisions, Firstmate coordinates an updated plan and brings it back for review.

When implementation starts, FM Linear sets the issue to **Building** and assigns Firstmate.
The plan and approval remain accessible from the issue.
Approval is permission to proceed, not evidence that a worker has already begun.

Plan approval does not authorize an unrelated deployment or approve the later pull request.
A material change to the plan may require another review under your configured workflow.

## How it works

FM Linear retrieves the review comment on its next poll, every 30 seconds by default.
It preserves the author, thread, and plan reference so the decision can be applied to the correct work.

<WorkflowFlow {...planFlow} />

1. **Capture the review.**
   FM Linear saves the comment and matches the Linear issue to its Firstmate task.
2. **Establish what is authorized.**
   Firstmate interprets the comment; FM Linear checks the reviewer's authority and the plan version the decision applies to.
   Ambiguous feedback needs clarification before proceeding.
3. **Coordinate implementation.**
   Firstmate schedules and dispatches the authorized work.
   Applicable workflow instructions belong in the assignment before it starts.
4. **Update Linear from progress.**
   FM Linear updates the status and assignee when implementation starts, while preserving the review history.
