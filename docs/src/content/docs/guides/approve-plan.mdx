## Motivation

Review the approach before the crew starts building.
In the recommended workflow, plans arrive in **Approve Plan**, assigned to you as captain or your configured reviewer.

## How to do it

Open the linked plan and check its scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, and open questions.
Reply in the review thread with changes or an explicit approval:

> I approve version 2 of this plan for implementation.
> Keep the proposed scope; we will review the deliverable before merging.

You can answer questions and approve in one reply.
If your requested changes make approval unclear, Firstmate asks for clarification.

## What happens

An authorized approval lets Firstmate begin implementing that plan.
If you request revisions, Firstmate brings the updated plan back for review.
When implementation starts, FM Linear moves the issue to **Building** and assigns Firstmate.

Approval applies to the reviewed plan, not a later PR merge or unrelated deployment.
Material changes may require another review.

## How it works

FM Linear retrieves your comment and checks the reviewer and plan version.
Firstmate interprets the decision and coordinates the next assignment.
