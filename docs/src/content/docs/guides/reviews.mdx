## Motivation

You want to clarify a request, change direction, answer a question, or ask the crew to revise its work.
You want to do that in Linear, with the discussion kept alongside the work it concerns.

## How to do it

Write a comment on the corresponding Linear issue.
When you are responding to an existing question or update, reply in that thread.
Describe the outcome you want and link a screenshot, plan section, or artifact when it helps.

For example:

> The invitation flow looks good so far.
> Please make the expiry clearer, add a way to revoke an invitation, and show me the revised screen before continuing.

You can put several feedback points in one comment.
You do not need a separate thread for every sentence or a new issue for each revision.

A request for changes does not approve the current artifact.
When you want to authorize the next step, explicitly [approve the plan](/guides/approve-plan/), [approve the deliverable](/guides/approve-deliverable/), or [authorize the merge](/guides/merge-pr/).

## What happens

Firstmate receives your comment with its issue and conversation context.
It interprets the feedback and decides how to respond or update the crew's assignment.
Firstmate replies directly in the same Linear thread, explaining the next action or asking for clarification.

The issue keeps the conversation and any revised materials together.
Receiving the comment, delivering it to Firstmate, and completing your requested change are distinct steps.
An acknowledgment does not mean the revision is finished, and unresolved feedback should remain visible while other work continues.

If you request a pause or cancellation, wait for Firstmate to confirm the execution change.
A comment or status edit alone does not prove that a running worker stopped.

## How it works

FM Linear retrieves comments from Linear, matches them to connected Firstmate tasks, and delivers them through the Firstmate adapter.
Firstmate supplies the response; FM Linear publishes it using the original thread reference.

<WorkflowFlow {...feedbackFlow} />

1. **Retrieve the comment.**
   FM Linear polls Linear every 30 seconds by default and saves incoming comments with their authors and thread context.
2. **Deliver the context.**
   The saved issue-to-task connection identifies the Firstmate work that the comment concerns.
   Pending delivery survives a restart; delivery confirmation is separate from resolving the request.
3. **Publish the response.**
   Firstmate interprets the feedback and coordinates the crew.
   FM Linear publishes the reported response in the same Linear thread.
