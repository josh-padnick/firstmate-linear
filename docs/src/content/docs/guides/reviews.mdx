## Motivation

Give feedback in Linear, alongside the work it concerns.

## How to do it

Comment on the connected issue.
When responding to a question or update, reply in that thread:

> Please make the invitation expiry clearer, add a way to revoke an invitation, and show me the revised screen before continuing.

Keep related feedback points in one comment.
Add screenshots or links when they help explain the change.

To authorize the next step, explicitly [approve the plan](/guides/approve-plan/), [approve the deliverable](/guides/approve-deliverable/), or [authorize the merge](/guides/merge-pr/).
Feedback alone does not grant approval.

## What happens

Firstmate interprets your feedback, coordinates the crew, and replies in the same Linear thread.
An acknowledgment means Firstmate received your request, not that the change is finished.
If you request a pause or cancellation, wait for Firstmate to confirm it stopped or changed the work.

For a lasting process change, ask Firstmate to update your [workflow configuration](/reference/configuration/#maintain-the-workflow).

## How it works

FM Linear checks Linear every 30 seconds by default, then delivers comments with their issue and thread context.
Firstmate may need more time to respond.
