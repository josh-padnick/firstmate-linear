## Motivation

Check the crew's result before it proceeds to validation.
In the recommended workflow, the issue arrives in **Approve Deliverable**, assigned to the configured reviewer.

## How to do it

Open the linked deliverable and check it against your request.
Review the reported checks and limitations.
If you [requested an interactive recap](/reference/workflow/#add-an-instruction-when-you-need-one), use it to walk through the work.

Reply in the review thread with changes or approve the version you reviewed:

> I approve this deliverable at the linked PR revision.
> Proceed with code validation; bring it back before merging.

## What happens

Firstmate arranges revisions or, after an authorized approval, validation.
When validation starts, FM Linear moves the issue to **Validating Code** and assigns Firstmate.
Findings and supporting links appear on the same issue.

**Deliverable approval does not authorize a merge.**
For non-code work, your workflow can define another next step or completion condition, such as accepting an investigation report.

## How it works

FM Linear delivers your comment with the deliverable reference and checks the reviewer's authority and the reviewed version.
Firstmate interprets the decision and coordinates the work.
